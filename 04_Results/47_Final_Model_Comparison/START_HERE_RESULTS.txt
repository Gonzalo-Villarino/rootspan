WHAT THE NUMBERS MEAN
The displayed new candidate is retention step 100, chosen for the best old-root
result among that attempt's checkpoints. It still failed the replacement gate.
On the assistant's sparse20_29 teaching labels, Endoderm recall increased from
31.7% to 94.2%; Periderm recall from 80.4% to 95.4%. This is TRAINING FIT.
On the reused MT25 root4 development reference, mean Periderm/Endoderm IoU fell
from 64.0% to 56.9% for RAW predictions. IoU measures overlap between predicted
and reference regions; it is not overall pixel accuracy. This regression is why
I did not replace the earlier model.
The earlier saved workflow, including its continuity rule, had tissue mean IoU
76.8% on this development reference. Do not compare that processed score with
raw scores as though only the network changed.
